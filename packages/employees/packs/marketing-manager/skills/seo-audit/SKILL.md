---
name: seo-audit
description: Audit a site's key pages for search basics and list the fixes worth doing first.
---
1. Fetch the home page and up to 5 key pages (pricing, main product or course pages, top blog post).
2. For each page check: title (unique, under ~60 chars, includes the main term), meta description, one clear H1, headings that follow the searcher's question, internal links to money pages, thin or duplicate content, missing alt text you can see in the HTML.
3. Search for the 3 main terms the business should rank for. Note who ranks above us and what their pages do better (format, depth, intent match).
4. Output a table: Page · Issue · Fix · Impact (H/M/L) · Effort (H/M/L). Sort by impact then effort.
5. End with the three fixes to do this week. Save the audit as reports/seo-audit-<date>.md.
