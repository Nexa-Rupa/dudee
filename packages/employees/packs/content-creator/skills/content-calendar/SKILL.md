description: Plan a week of content across channels tied to the current marketing goals.
---
1. Read memory for current goals, campaigns, launches and the brand voice. Ask the Marketing Manager (send_to_teammate) for this week's priorities if you have none.
2. Choose 3–5 themes that serve those goals. Mix formats: one deep piece, short posts, one video.
3. Lay out a table: Day · Channel · Format · Topic/angle · Goal it serves · Status.
4. Save as calendar/<week>.md and list which pieces you will draft first.
