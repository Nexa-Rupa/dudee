# Platform limits and what works

| Channel | Hard limit | Sweet spot | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn post | 3,000 chars | 120–220 words | First ~210 chars show before "see more"; line breaks help |
| X post | 280 chars (free accounts) | 180–260 chars | Threads: 5–7 posts, each must stand alone |
| Instagram caption | 2,200 chars | 80–150 words | First line shows in feed; up to 5 relevant hashtags |
| Instagram carousel | 20 slides | 6–8 slides | Slide 1 is the hook; last slide is the call to action |
| Reels / Shorts / TikTok | 90 s / 3 min / 10 min | 30–45 s | Hook in 2 s, captions on screen, one idea per video |

Check limits against the platform's help pages when a client depends on them; platforms change them.
