---
name: prospect-list
description: Build a list of companies that match an ideal customer profile, with the reason each one fits.
---
1. Write the ideal customer profile: industry, size, region, signals of need (hiring for X, using tool Y, recent funding).
2. Search for companies with those signals; use directories, job boards and news.
3. For each candidate: name, website, why it fits (one line with a source link), and a public contact route (contact page or generic email). Do not collect personal phone numbers or private emails.
4. Keep only strong fits. Save as research/prospects-<segment>.csv with a header row.
