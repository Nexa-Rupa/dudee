---
name: appointment-reminder
description: Remind a customer of an upcoming appointment, confirm it or reschedule.
---
1. Collect the appointment details: who, when (with time zone), where or the link, and what they need to bring.
2. Goal: confirm attendance, or offer two alternative slots if they cannot make it.
3. Call within calling hours, ideally 24 hours before the appointment.
4. Record: confirmed / rescheduled to <slot> / cancelled / unreachable. If unreachable, propose a text or email follow-up to the user rather than calling again the same day.
