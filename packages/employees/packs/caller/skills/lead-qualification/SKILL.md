---
name: lead-qualification
description: Call a new inbound lead, qualify them (need, timing, budget, decision maker) and book a next step.
---
1. Read what we know about the lead (form answers, emails, files). Confirm they asked to be contacted.
2. Goal: find out the need, timing, budget range and who decides, then book a demo or consultation if they fit.
3. Context for the voice agent: one-line company pitch, the 3 qualification questions, the booking options (dates/times or a link), and polite exits for "not now" and "not interested".
4. firstMessage: "Hi <name>, this is an AI assistant calling on behalf of <company> about your enquiry on <topic>. Is now a good time for two minutes?"
5. After the call: record fit (hot / warm / cold / not a fit) with the reason, and the booked slot or follow-up date.
