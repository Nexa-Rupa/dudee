**dudee 2.1.0** adds connections and daily social posting: hire a Social Media Manager, add your accounts, and she posts every day, with images and videos she makes herself.

## Install or upgrade

```bash
npm install -g dudee@2.1.0
dudee ui
```

Your workspace (`.dude/`) carries over; new tables and files are added in place.

## What's new

**Daily autopost** (new role: Social Media Manager)
- Add X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, Pinterest or any other site under **Connections**
- Pick the time, days, format (text, image, video or a mix), a brief and topics to rotate
- Every day she picks an angle, writes the copy, makes the media, signs in, publishes and logs the link
- "Ask me first" shows one approval card (in the app or on Telegram); "Post without asking" goes straight out
- **Post now** runs today's post at once; **Recent posts** shows what went out

**Videos made in code, on your machine**
- Remotion: the employee writes a JSON list of scenes (title, bullets, image, clip, quote, stat, call to action, captions, music, voiceover) and `video_render` turns it into an MP4
- HyperFrames: HTML + GSAP motion design, checked and rendered to MP4 (needs FFmpeg)
- Voiceovers with ElevenLabs or OpenAI text-to-speech

**Login vault**
- Social logins and two-step secrets are encrypted in `.dude/vault.json`
- `vault_fill` types them into the employee's browser; the model never sees them, and any tool output that echoes one is scrubbed

**Apps over OAuth (Composio)**
- Save one Composio project key, click **Connect** on any of 500+ apps, finish the sign-in in your browser
- Employees use them as `apps__*` tools; lookups run at once, actions ask first

**Telegram**
- Pair a chat with a code, message employees by name, approve with buttons, answer questions by replying, get routine reports

**App servers (MCP) from the web app**
- Paste a URL or a command, test it, add it

See [docs/CONNECTIONS.md](../CONNECTIONS.md) for setup.
